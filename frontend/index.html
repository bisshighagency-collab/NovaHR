Everything you need, from hiring to payday

One simple app for every part of workforce management — no spreadsheets, no scattered tools.

01

Attendance that matches your sign-in sheet

Your team keeps signing the paper sheet; you record only the late arrivals and absences. Each employee's dashboard clock shows their own service hours and break time, set by you. Late or absent? Employees justify it with a reason and a document, like a doctor's note, for you to approve.

02

Payroll that runs itself

Every employee gets their own salary and their own late/absence deduction rates — set once by the admin, no standard pay scale forced on anyone. Payroll is calculated automatically from real attendance records at the end of each month, ready for admin review and disbursement.

03

One admin dashboard for everything

Active employees, who's present today, open job postings, candidates awaiting review, payroll pending approval, and today's late or absent staff — all in one view that updates itself. Broadcast a company-wide announcement in one click when something changes.

04

AI that actually helps, on the job

A company-trained assistant answers staff questions about leave, pay, and policy instantly, walks new hires through onboarding, and conducts the first round of interviews for open roles — scoring candidates automatically so your team reviews shortlists, not resumes.
